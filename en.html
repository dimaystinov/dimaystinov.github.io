Profile

I prepare school teams for autonomous soccer and humanoid robot all-round competitions.

I lead the Humanoid Robotics Laboratory and the MechTech Robot Soccer club. I graduated from the Faculty of Physics at Moscow State University in 2015.

At RoboKit, students assemble and tune robots, program behaviour, work with computer vision and test their solutions in competitions. MIPT and StarKit developed the platforms; the school team focuses on tuning, programming and integration.

Autonomous behaviour

Python, behaviour trees, ball search, approach and kicking.

Computer vision

OpenCV, image processing and training object detectors.

Motion and simulation

Roki-2 motion tuning, Elsiros / Webots and algorithm testing.

Teaching

Practical classes, team projects and preparation for FIRA and RoboCup.
